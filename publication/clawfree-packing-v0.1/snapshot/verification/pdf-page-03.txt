3 Structure and metric of the expansion
Lemma 3.1. For every k ≥ 3, Dk is a connected simple cubic graph. Its triangle expansion
Gk is connected, cubic, claw-free and diamond-free, with 12k vertices and 18k edges.
Proof. Within a diamond, ai and bi have neighbors equal to the other base vertex and both
tips. The tips have neighborhoods
NDk (pi) = {ai, bi, qi− 1}, N Dk (qi) = {ai, bi, pi+1}. (2)
All these neighbors are distinct for k ≥ 3. The edges in ( 1) have no loops or repetitions. Every
diamond is connected, and the joining edges form a ring of the diamonds, so Dk is connected.
More generally, for any simple cubic D, a vertex (v, w) of T (D) has two neighbors in Tv
and the third neighbor (w, v) outside Tv. These three neighbors are distinct, and the ﬁrst two
are adjacent. Hence the expansion is simple, cubic and claw-free. A path in D lifts to a walk
between its replacement triangles, and every triangle is connected; therefore connectedness of
D implies connectedness of T (D). There are 3|V (Dk)|= 12 k incidences, and cubicity gives
|E(Gk)|= 3(12k)/2 = 18 k.
The edges between distinct replacement triangles form a matching. No such edge can
belong to a triangle: a hypothetical third vertex would require one of its endpoints to have a
second neighbor outside its own replacement triangle. Thus every triangle of the expansion is
one of the Tv. A diamond contains two triangles sharing an edge, whereas the Tv are vertex-
disjoint. Consequently Gk is diamond-free. This last observation concerns the expanded
graph; the core Dk certainly contains diamonds.
Lemma 3.2. Let D be a simple cubic graph.
1. If uv ∈ E(D), then every pair x ∈ Tu, y ∈ Tv is joined by a walk of length at most three
in T (D).
2. If x = (v, w), u ∈ ND(w) \ {v} and y ∈ Tu, then x and y are joined by a walk of length
at most four in T (D).
Proof. For the ﬁrst assertion, use the internal step from x to (u, v), then the edge (u, v)(v, u),
and ﬁnally the internal step from (v, u) to y. An internal step is omitted when its endpoints
coincide. Each internal step has length at most one.
