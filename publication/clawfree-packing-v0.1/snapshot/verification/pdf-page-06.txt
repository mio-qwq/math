7 Discussion
The obstruction has two distinct components. Triangle expansion converts short walks into
restrictions on a core coloring. The diamond ring then ampliﬁes the local private-neighbor
restriction into a global parity contradiction. This explains why the witness extends to arbi-
trarily large orders without a search or a new distance calculation for each graph.
The core condition is a useful way to test a proposed (1,1,3,3,4)-coloring route, but we
have not proved that it is suﬃcient. Even ring lengths escape the ﬁnal parity contradiction;
that fact alone does not prove their expanded graphs colorable. An explicit k = 4 coloring is
checked as a positive control in the supplement. We do not claim a classiﬁcation of all admis-
sible cores, the smallest counterexample, or a solution to other packing coloring problems.
The ﬁxed formal witness is enough to give a negative answer to the original universal
question. Extending the Lean development to cyclic indices of arbitrary odd order would
formalize the family theorem, but is not needed for the mathematical proof presented here.
The source’s positive results for weaker palettes remain part of the existing literature and are
not counted as additional new results of this paper.
