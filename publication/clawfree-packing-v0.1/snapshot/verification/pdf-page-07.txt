A Exact replay and certiﬁcate conventions
The ﬁxed JSON certiﬁcate uses the numbering given in Section 6. Undirected edge pairs are
stored once, with the smaller endpoint ﬁrst, in lexicographic order. The incidence labels and
the palette are part of the certiﬁcate, not descriptive metadata that a checker may ignore.
The following pseudocode reconstructs the object.
For each cyclic index i, add the ﬁve diamond edges and qipi+1. For each core vertex v, sort
its three neighbors and list the incidences (v, w). Join all three pairs within Tv, and join
each incidence to its reciprocal (w, v). Reject loops, repeated edges and a mismatching
certiﬁcate. Compute BFS from every expanded vertex.
The bounded structural replay checks cubicity, connectivity, claw-freeness, unique replace-
ment triangles, and all the metric bounds used in Proposition 4.1. It checks the tip-neighbor
formulas at every cyclic index without enumerating the full space of original colorings. A
separate independent checker reconstructs the graph without importing the author’s veriﬁer.
The ﬁnite regressions are checks of the implementation, not proofs of the all-odd- k statement.
The controls include a missing edge, a corrupted incidence label, a changed palette, an
invalid equal-low-label coloring on adjacent vertices, a valid (1,1,3,3,3) coloring of G3, and
a valid (1,1,3,3,4) coloring of G4. Every positive coloring is tested against distances in the
entire expanded graph. The historical exhaustive veriﬁer remains available as an optional
command; it is not part of the bounded publication replay.
For Lean, use the supplied lean-toolchain and lake-manifest.json; the evidence uses

d13f23b723b8a846827a245b89c10fc7d3f11612.
The REPRODUCE.md ﬁle gives the exact commands, including the standalone source-root option.

SHA256. Installing Lean from an empty cache is a separate environment step and is not
claimed as a tested part of this package replay.
