- Check `git status`, local history, and `git fetch origin` before editing or publishing. Inspect partner branches and commits. Preserve uncommitted work and avoid another agent's owned files.

- Distinguish finite certificates, polynomial interpretation, algebra construction, cochain statements, and the full homological realization. Do not claim a complete ARC proof from a supporting result.
