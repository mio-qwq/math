# A's candidate claim

## Exact scope

Ficarra–Moradi, *Monomial ideals whose all matching powers are Cohen-Macaulay*,
[arXiv:2410.01666v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.01666v2), 24 April 2025,
Question 4.2. Checked 10 October 2026.

For each n >= 5, among finite simple n-vertex graphs without isolated vertices,
excluding complete graphs and Cohen-Macaulay forests, are exactly two
isomorphism classes left whose edge ideals have Cohen-Macaulay quotients for
every nonzero matching power? Work here first uses the field Q.

An edge ideal has generators x_i x_j for edges ij. Its kth matching power is
generated by products from k vertex-disjoint edges, for 1 <= k <= nu(G).

## Refutation and verification plan (our own)

To give a negative answer it suffices to exhibit, at one common n, three
pairwise nonisomorphic admissible graphs. For each graph and each k reconstruct
the simplicial complex

    Delta_k = { F subset V : nu(G[F]) < k }.

Check its Cohen-Macaulay property via exact reduced homology of every link,
or give a directly checkable pure shelling. Verify the excluded graph classes
and isomorphism separation independently. A numerical guess or a pure complex
alone is not a positive certificate.

## Source-state screening

Crupi–Ficarra, [arXiv:2503.08521v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.08521v2),
Theorem 2.3, adds the linear-resolution requirement to each power; that stronger
assumption does not settle this target. Later matching-power work, including
[arXiv:2607.04964v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04964), was screened.
No same-scope resolution was located; this is a bounded search, not a guarantee
of open status or novelty. An important candidate will trigger another search.

The claim covers this exact question and direct matching-power CM searches,
not all graph ideals or simplicial complexes. No external construction has
been adopted. General matching, simplicial homology, and graph enumeration
tools may be used with attribution.
