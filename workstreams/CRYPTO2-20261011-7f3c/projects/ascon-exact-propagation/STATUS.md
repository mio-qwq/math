## Precise target

Use the standard 320-bit Ascon permutation's two consecutive rounds,
including their constants, with a uniform unconstrained initial state.
For every fixed input difference A, intermediate substitution-output
difference B, and second substitution-output difference C, determine the
exact trail probability and its deviation from the product of local DDT
probabilities. Establish a verifiable rank condition for equality, and
produce a full-width counterexample if equality is not universal.

This is our precise bounded subproblem of the error-quantification direction
in Hu–Niu–Wang, EUROCRYPT 2026 / ePrint 2026/358, Section 7.4. That paper
does not assert universal exactness, and a failure of a two-round product
will NOT refute its stated heuristic or its reported distinguishers.
NIST SP 800-232 (2025) fixes the actual permutation. Arbitrary uniform
permutation input is not Ascon-AEAD initialization with fixed IV/key.

Candidate mechanism: exploit affine derivatives of the quadratic S-box;
eliminate uniform values in inactive first-round columns by binary rank;
count only remaining constraints on the exact S-box output fibers.
This is distinct from APN gamma/Sidon periods, ChaCha local correlations,
the excluded SHA3 model, and our prior circulant RD quotient proof.
