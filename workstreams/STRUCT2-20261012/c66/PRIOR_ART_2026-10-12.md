# C66 quantitative work: source overlap and search limits

Checked 2026-10-12 UTC (2026-10-13 in Singapore). This is a bounded
comparison of accessible public material, not a priority certificate.

## Original problem

Bartlomiej Bychawski, *Constructions of graphs with any possible two-fold
automorphism and automorphism groups*, [arXiv:2406.06267v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.06267v1),
Conjecture 6.6, asks for an expectation limit. Its reduced-graph definition
and the all-labelled-graphs wording have the domain issue already recorded
in the frozen C-audit candidate. The full ordered-pair group, its projection
on all graphs, and the projection conditioned on reduced graphs are
different random variables. No claim below silently identifies them.

## Existing publicly posted proof claim

The [MathDB entry](https://mathdb.com/p/363470/conjecture-on-the-expected-size-of-the-two-fold-automorphism)
was read in full through its public text, including the actual submitted
proof and reduced-domain conclusion. It gives the full-group expectation
limit for every fixed edge probability and obtains the projected and
conditional variants. Its method uses edge-equality components and support
summation. This establishes substantial overlap with a proposed resolution
of the bare limit; that resolution must not be advertised as new here.

The entry labels the submission unverified. That is its stated review
status, not evidence of a mathematical error. The displayed component,
support and summation argument was checked locally and no decisive gap was
identified. A local assessment is not the community's independent acceptance.
The page does not supply a second exponential-order coefficient table or
a sharp sparse `p=c log(n)/n` constant for the full-group expectation.

## Exact comparison of the new candidate

| Statement | Recorded earlier scope | This continuation |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed `0<p<1`, expected full TF group tends to one | Public MathDB proof claim | Reproved as a corollary; no newness claim |

| Effective second-order error | Not in those two inspected sources | `R_n <= 200000000*n^6*2^(-3n)` for `n>=1024` |
| Sparse full-group expectation | Fixed-p statement does not supply the sharp range | Convergence for fixed `c>4`, divergence for fixed `0<c<4` |
| Sparse reduced/twin-free critical window | Classical isolated-vertex mechanism | Explicit reduced-domain consequence; no newness of the Poisson mechanism claimed |

The new selection lemma uses one orientation of every chosen source edge,
outdegree at most one, and the Bernoulli two-coordinate norm bound. Its
sparse summation is stronger than the coarse fixed-p estimate in the
inspected MathDB submission. The rare empty-graph lower bound is elementary.
Those differences motivate a quantitative manuscript; they do not rule out
an equivalent result in unindexed, unpublished or differently named work.

## Search coverage and limitations

Two web search engines were queried using Bychawski/Conjecture 6.6,
two-fold automorphism/expected size/random graph, and sparse-threshold
terminology. The exact MathDB page and the original arXiv abstract/full-text
links were opened. Institutional records on the classical two-fold group
were also returned. Several long exact-phrase queries produced unrelated
results, so their recall was poor. No claim of an exhaustive search is made.

The present searches did not identify another source with the same
second-order coefficients or sharp full-group sparse constant. Before a
priority claim or journal submission, search ordinary bipartite-matrix
stabilizers, random incidence matrices, permutation-pair invariance and
Burnside expectation asymptotics, and inspect any matching source fully.
No author was contacted; no automated messages or external submission were
made. Full third-party papers are not redistributed in this directory.
