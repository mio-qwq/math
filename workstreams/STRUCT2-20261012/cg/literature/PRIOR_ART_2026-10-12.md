# Prior-art scope for odd claw-graph TF-cousin counting

Checked on 2026-10-12. This is a bounded literature comparison, not a proof of
mathematical correctness or a certification of historical priority.

## Finding

The inspected public sources do not supply the proposed all-odd exact
classification. They do establish prior credit for the second companion
construction. A possible new contribution is therefore the universal upper
bound and complete classification, provided these are proved independently.
The newness of the known reflection construction must not be claimed.

Mizzi v3 Definition 6.1 uses a cycle on 6n vertices and n claws, with
leaf (i,j) attached to positions i+jn and i+jn+3n. Its Proposition 6.1
concerns the displayed companion obtained by splitting that cycle. The
unnumbered question in Section 7 concerns the complete count instead.
That section states that the reflection fold contains triangles and gives
at least two cousins for odd n >= 3. The case n=1 has one cousin.
The folding/conjugacy correspondence is attributed in Remark 4.7 to
Pacco--Scapellato, with Bychawski's refinement discussed in Remark 4.8.

The citation to the Bolzano paper in Mizzi v3 identifies the system used
for a communicated construction; the cited paper is not itself a CG
classification paper. Full downloaded HTML text for both Bolzano versions
has zero occurrences of `claw`, `Mizzi`, `cousin`, `strongly switching`,
or `dihedral`. The headings and the case studies were also checked; keyword
absence alone is not the basis for the scope judgment.

## Subsequent-work search and its limits

Multiple general and primary-source searches combined the exact paper
identifier, `TF-cousins`, `claw`, `CG(n)`, `strongly switching`,
`classification`, `exactly two`, `24n`, and the authors' names. No separate
public source proving the claimed count, cover automorphism-group upper
bound, or full involution classification was located. Search results were
mostly Mizzi's paper and unrelated uses of the words; absence from search
is weak negative evidence.

The inspected [Rozhoň publication page](https://vaclavrozhon.github.io/research/papers/)
contains no CG result, but explicitly says it is less current than his
Scholar page. [Šámal's institutional page](https://iuuk.mff.cuni.cz/~samal/)
did not expose such a result. These pages cannot certify the absence of
recent work. The public Bolzano landing URL returned an application shell;
no CG-specific public transcript was located or inspected. No authors were
contacted.

Accordingly, the defensible contribution description is: an exact
classification candidate extending a previously public lower-bound
construction, with no equivalent full result found within the stated
search. A claim that nobody had previously solved it is not supported.
