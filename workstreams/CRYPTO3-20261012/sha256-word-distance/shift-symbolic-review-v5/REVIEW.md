## Independent verification of the algebra

The review checker imports no author code and uses no rational arithmetic,
Gaussian division or solver verdict. It constructs its own original rows
from the five lagged terms, then reads the author certificate as data.

| Support | Rows and columns | Checked coordinates of U*A | Reversible operations | Unit pivots | Free columns |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 9 | 48 by 39 | 1,872 | 83 | 23 | 16 |
| 12 | 48 by 39 | 1,872 | 114 | 25 | 14 |

All recorded operations are row swaps, negations, or addition of an integer
multiple of a **different** row. Each has an explicit integral inverse,
which remains an inverse after reduction modulo `2^32`. The independent
checker replays them on A and on the 48-by-48 identity, verifies the stored
U and transformed B, multiplies U*A directly in every coordinate, and
replays the inverse operations to recover A and the identity exactly.

Every pivot column of B is a unit coordinate column; all rows after the
pivot rows are exactly zero over Z. Thus these pivot rows freely generate
the entire row module over each stated ring. Reducing the independently
constructed targets with those unit rows gives the author's exact free
residuals:

```
L9-(F9+G22-G57) = -E22+E24-E29-E36-E43-E59,
L12-(-G13-G29) = -E31.
```

Both identities were checked directly against the reconstructed original
rows, independently of U. Each residual has zero pivot coordinates and
a coefficient +1 or -1 in a free coordinate. A row-module vector with
all pivot coordinates zero must have all pivot-row coefficients zero,
so it must be the zero vector. The displayed residuals are nonzero over
Z and over `Z/(2^32)`, proving nonmembership without a field assumption.

For support 12 the G13 column is identically zero in all 48 original rows:
the minimum G index is `16-2=14`. Its target coefficient -1 is itself a
direct nonmembership obstruction. The reviewer also checked
`E29=d29-d13` exactly. These are free-interface facts; G13 has not been
shown to be a freely realizable derivative of actual words.

## Separate model-based proof and the precise limitation

The certificate models were checked independently against all original
rows, not merely against the reduced rows:

| Support | Integer target value | Target modulo 2^32 | Integer equations | Modular equations | All active interfaces |
|---:|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| 9 | -14,329,223 | 4,280,638,073 | All 48 zero | All 48 zero | All 39 nonzero in both rings |
| 12 | -59,778 | 4,294,907,518 | All 48 zero | All 48 zero | All 39 nonzero in both rings |

The stored modular values are exactly the reductions of the integer lifts.
The checker evaluates both versions directly. In either ring, any row
combination evaluates to zero on a vector annihilated by every original
row. The nonzero target evaluations therefore supply an independent
separating proof of nonmembership, without needing the rank result at all.

For real schedules, `d_-1=d15-G13-d8-F0`; support substitution gives the
two targets above. If the applicable target were zero, the actual common
backward word would allow the corresponding one-word right shift. The
reviewer checked `S9+1=S0`, `S12+1=S6`, the inactive cropped tail, and
retention of distinct initial inputs. The candidate does **not** prove
that a real pair can have a nonzero target. Its countermodels satisfy
free additive equations, with no actual words realizing the simultaneous
sigma differences, no common 16-word base-message construction, and no
full expanded pair. Nonmembership is not a converse to actual derivative
semantics. Both reverse-implication questions remain open.
