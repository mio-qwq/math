# B reservation: circulants with diameter two and monophonic position number two

2026-10-10 10:35UTC. Exact original: James Tuite, Elias John Thomas and Ullas Chandran S.V., On some extremal position problems for graphs, Ars Mathematica Contemporanea25(1)(2025),#P1.09, DOI10.26493/1855-3974.3094.bc6, Section4 Conjecture4.5, printed page17. The original preprint is arXiv2106.06827v3 (8February2022). The final journal PDF and current preprint history were directly read.

For every integer n>=11, there exists a finite simple undirected CIRCULANT graph G with |V(G)|=n, diameter2 and monophonic position number mp(G)=2. Here an mp-set contains no three distinct vertices lying on one induced path, not merely a shortest path. A positive proof must give a graph for EVERY n and cover EVERY triple by an induced path. A refutation needs an order n for which NO circulant has both properties; one failed generator family does not suffice.
