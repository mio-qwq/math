## Local-girth packing coloring: active all-length proof candidate

Original: Ayman El Zein and Maidoun Mortada, *Impact of local girth on the
S-packing coloring of k-saturated subcubic graphs*, arXiv:2603.25113v1,
26 March 2026, Section 5 Conjecture 1.
https://arxiv.org/html/2603.25113v1#S5

Full hypotheses: finite, simple, undirected; maximum degree at most three;
degree-three vertices independent; each degree-three vertex belongs to a cycle
of length at most four. No minimum-degree, connectedness or planarity assumption
is added. A color-i class requires pairwise distance STRICTLY greater than i.
The displayed g3 maximum is used; imprecise surrounding prose and the claimed
degree-two-neighbor equivalence are not substituted for the formal hypotheses.

Version, errata, later work, author-page and theorem-overlap checks are in
`packing_gate/literature_gate_2026-10-10.md`; exact queries in its companion log.
Current abstract lists v1 only. An August 2026 author follow-on concerns a
separate claw-free S-packing conjecture and does not settle this target.

Known overlap: all-even opposite-port square necklaces already follow from
Lemdani–Abbas–Ferme, Filomat 34(10), 2020, Proposition 2.8 and subgraph
monotonicity. The proposed new argument must cover odd/mixed connectors and
all graph components, not rebrand the even subclass as new.

Gate conclusion: reasonable to investigate the all-length construction; no full
primary resolution located in this bounded pass. Some author bibliography
access was incomplete. Historical originality remains unconfirmed.
