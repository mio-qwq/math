## Newly implemented adversarial verifier

`audit_orbit_from_ordered_pairs.py` builds every eligible edge-selection class as the closure of ordered vertex pairs under **two raw generators**: endpoint reversal and the original TF action (u,v)->(q(u),q^{-1}(v)). It **does not** construct graph edges from the proof's claimed gcd-sum classes.

For each allowed class, it independently rejects (i) diagonal edges in A and (ii) edges A(u,q(u)) that become forbidden diagonal loops in B=Aq. The resulting labelled adjacency matrices are checked for symmetry, zero loops and the full TF biconditional.

It then enforces **both** graphs connected, nonbipartite and vertex-determining; checks nonisomorphism with exact NetworkX VF2 (degree/triangle multisets are merely early filters); verifies the full **2n by 2n canonical double-cover adjacency mapping** for each qualifying pair; and enumerates actual simple cycles of every possible odd k and 2k using DFS, requiring two **vertex-disjoint** k-cycle vertex sets in one graph. This final test **does not** use C's proposed congruence-cycle lift.

Three deliberate negative controls are rejected: nonbijective q, inserted target-graph loop, and a single changed original-graph edge.

## Review of the universal mathematical argument

The numerical results are **not** a proof. Separately, the C universal manuscript's mathematical steps were checked against the source definitions:

1. Normalize TF isomorphism as B(u,v)=A(u,q(v)), where q=beta^{-1}alpha, after relabelling H by alpha.
2. Symmetry gives A(u,v)=A(q(u),q^{-1}(v)). Within an odd q-orbit an edge would produce an A-loop; within an even q-orbit an internal edge of odd displacement would eventually yield A(x,q(x))=1, hence a B-loop. This exhausts all internal edges.
3. Between distinct orbits, TF edge orbits are constant-sum classes modulo gcd(m_i,m_j) by CRT, and B shifts every class by -1.
4. If the graph on *even-length* orbit classes is bipartite, choose orbit rotations satisfying 2e_i=1 on odd-primary parts and e_i equal to quotient colors on even-primary parts. CRT yields e_i+e_j=1 mod gcd for every adjacent orbit pair; hence A and B are ordinarily isomorphic via the full adjacency biconditional.
5. Otherwise a simple odd quotient cycle on distinct even orbits exists. Its selected gcd classes have odd alternating closure sum D in exactly one of A,B. If D even, a cyclic lift exists; a second disjoint lift uses d=2^(r-1)*oddpart(L), choosing the first orbit at minimal r=v2(m). The sole nonzero closing correction is 2d, divisible by the closing-edge gcd. If D odd, two traversals under x->D-x use two distinct residues in each *even* orbit and form a simple doubled cycle.
