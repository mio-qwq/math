## Exact initial question and source gate

Sidorenko's graph conjecture: for every finite simple bipartite H and every nonempty finite simple graph G,

`t(H,G) >= t(K2,G) ^ |E(H)|`, where `t(H,G)=hom(H,G)/|V(G)|^|V(H)|` and hom counts ALL adjacency-preserving vertex maps, including noninjective maps.

A strict finite counterexample supplies H, a bipartition, G, exact hom counts and a strict reversed inequality after clearing positive denominators. A counterexample to a hypergraph, directed, signed-kernel, stronger weighted or forcing version is not automatically a graph counterexample. If using a rational nonnegative symmetric step kernel, write and prove the bridge to the original graph formulation; do not omit density normalization, diagonal-block behavior or the strict-gap argument.
