# Coordinate augmentations and the eighteen-label kernel

`TwentyDimAugmentation.lean` proves that the e and f coordinates are actual unital ring homomorphisms from `TableAlgebra q` to R. Their combined map to R × R is surjective. The key coefficient identities are checked over the 400 basis pairs for each output coordinate and transported to genuine polynomial and specialized constants.

The kernel consists exactly of vectors whose coordinates 0 and 8 vanish, and exactly of the already defined `radicalSum U` vectors. The equivalence is proved by the injectivity and coverage of the eighteen-label enumeration. The kernel is closed under addition, negation and multiplication by arbitrary algebra elements on either side; the displayed span is closed under its actual product.

The results hold over every commutative characteristic-two ring and every parameter. All twelve audits compile without warnings and use standard axioms. No large cochain enumeration is repeated. This source does not assert nilpotence or identify the kernel with the Jacobson radical.
